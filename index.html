v0.1.0n8nAI + human-in-the-loopWhatsApp

Garage Quote Agent

Small car-repair garages lose customers because nobody answers "how much?" while the mechanics are under a car. This n8n automation answers price requests from WhatsApp, a web form or a webhook. AI reads the message, the garage's own price book sets the price, and the owner approves anything uncertain from Telegram.

Repo & workflows → Case study Decisions

Product calls

  • AI reads, rules price. The model never states a price.
  • Confidence gate: automatic replies only when every check passes. Otherwise the owner sees the reasons.
  • Channel rules shape the design: WhatsApp's 24-hour window decides when follow-ups go out.

Status

22 of 22 test scenarios passed in n8n with stand-in services. No pilot results yet.

KostasTravel

A hotel booking site and a room-mapping explorer on the same travel API. The site shows what mapping and content quality look like to the guest who pays. The explorer shows the machinery: which supplier room names land on which master room, which rooms never get a price, and which records look alike.

Live demo → Case study Repo

What I found

  • 533 of 533 supplier rates were resolved to a master room. Coverage was not the problem.
  • Precision and content were. One hotel stored the same room twice, and every price was attached to the empty copy, the one with no amenities.

LiteAPI sandbox · Kraków, 40 hotels analysed in depth · 13 Nov 2026. Free hosting may take a minute to wake up.

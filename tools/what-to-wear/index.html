The rules

Based on the "feels like" temperature, which includes wind and humidity. Each window uses its coldest hour, so you are not caught out.

24°C or moreT-shirt
18 to 24°CT-shirt, with a light layer to carry
12 to 18°CLong sleeves and a light jacket
6 to 12°CSweater and a jacket
0 to 6°CSweater and a warm coat
Below 0°CWinter coat, hat, gloves and scarf
Rain chance 50% or moreUmbrella or rain jacket
Rain chance 30 to 50%Pack an umbrella just in case
Wind 30 km/h or moreWindproof outer layer
8°C or more between windowsLayers you can take off
